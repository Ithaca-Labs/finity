title: Trust boundaries
description: Three processes. One narrow handoff.
---

Secrets stay out of the agent transcript. The Ledger signs authority; it never signs individual x402 payments.

<Cards>
  <Card title="A / buyer — Pi agent" description="Receives intents, decisions, receipts, and service output. Never sees keys or plaintext credentials." />
  <Card title="B / broker — finityd" description="Runs discovery, negotiation, policy evaluation, capability minting, and trace building." />
  <Card title="C / vault — vault-worker" description="Decrypts sealed bundles for a lease, injects credentials, enforces egress, and pays." />
</Cards>
