---
title: How it works
description: Authority starts on a screen only a human can approve.
---

The Principal clear-signs a human-readable Agent Mandate on a physical Ledger. Finity turns that authority into a deterministic path: discover, quote, evaluate, reserve, pay, and record.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Set the boundary

Allowed providers, services, methods, assets, data rules, and spend limits.

</Step>
<Step>

### Find a service

Signed service manifests and signed quotes are discovered by the broker.

</Step>
<Step>

### Evaluate the quote

The pure policy engine checks every predicate and collects every failure.

</Step>
<Step>

### Pay and call

A capability scopes the request before the vault injects credentials.

</Step>
<Step>

### Leave a trail

Decision, payment, usage, and reconciliation receipts are hash-chained to HCS.

</Step>
</Steps>
