---
title: Policy outcomes
description: Every result has a reason.
---

Unknown, missing, or malformed policy and payment inputs fail closed as `STATE_UNAVAILABLE`. Otherwise, the outcome is one of three typed states.

<Callout type="success" title="authorized">
  No failing predicates. A single-use capability can be minted and the request can settle.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn" title="escalation">
  Only spend limits fail. The agent can propose an amendment for fresh Ledger approval.
</Callout>

<Callout type="error" title="refused">
  Any other failure. No payment, capability, or credential access is allowed.
</Callout>
